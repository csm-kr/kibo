# Sources

Every external fact KIBO's public material may use: the claim, the number, the publisher, the title, the date, the URL and the exact place in the document. Written for pitch deck v2 on 2026-10-14 from primary documents read on 2026-10-13 (laws through the law.go.kr Open API, current text and amendment notices; statistics from the agencies' own PDF and Excel files), plus two Google pages read on 2026-10-13 and 10-14. Secondary sources (press) are marked **[secondary]**.

Rules:
- Use a number only with its source line (publisher, year) and, where the medium allows, a link.
- Quotes below are our English translations of the Korean originals (marked "tr."); check the original at the given location before quoting a translation as exact wording. Do not round in a way that changes the claim (51.4% is not "51.5%").
- Korean titles stay as proper names, with an English translation in brackets.
- KIBO's own measurements are not here: they come from `evidence/`, `evals/results/` and `spikes/*/results/` (see `docs/TESTING.md` and `docs/plan/NUMBERS.md`).

The "Deck v2" column says where the fact appears in `videos/deck-v2/` (slide number), or "not used".
