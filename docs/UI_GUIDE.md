# UI Guide

## Design principles
1. **Look like a real kiosk.** Follow the familiar fast-food kiosk flow (below); our job is to make it easier, not novel.
2. **The phone is the main screen; the kiosk is the mirror.** Everything needed to order (cart, total, confirm, order number) is readable on the phone alone.
3. **Elderly-first.** If Sunja (72) can do it, everyone can. One task per screen, plain words, undo and call-staff always reachable.

Reference prototype: `design/prototype/Main.dc.html` (Claude Design). Menu photos: `design/menu/`. Logos: `design/brand/`.

**Canvas.** The kiosk is designed on a 1080×1920 portrait canvas and scaled to fit the screen (`/kiosk`, `/demo`); every kiosk size in this guide is on that canvas. The prototype draws the kiosk 596px wide (about 0.55×), so its kiosk pixel values are not the spec. Phone sizes are CSS px at 390×844.

## Kiosk flow to follow
Attract screen (language, large-text / low-screen button, "talk (QR)" and "touch") → dine-in / take-out photo cards → left category rail + photo menu grid (badges, kcal, sold out) → "Make it a set?" (single / set / large set) → side → drink → ingredients and quantity → dessert suggestion (once per order) → bottom cart bar (preview, clear all, order) → order review (quantity) → payment method → card insert and approval → receipt? → order number.

Copy follows real kiosk wording: "Make it a set?", "Insert your card below", "Print a receipt?". The kiosk starts in Korean and offers English on K1 (`src/lib/i18n/kiosk.ts`, ticket machines use ticket words), and the airport rail ticket machine also 日本語 (Japanese ticket-machine wording: 乗車券, 大人, こども, お支払い, 領収書, 受付番号; won as "10,500ウォン"); the phone uses the user's language. With three languages the K1 language row takes its own line and [Large text] goes under it; K1 scrolls rather than clips when larger text and WCAG 1.4.12 spacing make it taller than the canvas. Japanese on the kiosk canvas wraps at phrase ends (`word-break: auto-phrase` where supported, else normal with `line-break: strict`), and Japanese copy is chosen so a phrase fits its line (a 3-column option card holds 6 characters at 40px).

## Anti-patterns (do not use)
| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| `backdrop-filter: blur()` glass panels | Generic AI-template look; hurts contrast for low vision |
| Gradient text, gradient orbs, neon glow animations | Decoration that reads as AI template, not kiosk |
| "Powered by AI" badges | No value to the user; say what happened instead ("Added by AI") |
| Identical `rounded-2xl` on everything | Template feel; use radius by role (phone: tiles 18px, sheets 26px; kiosk canvas: tiles 32px, sheets 48px; pills full) |
| Emoji as icons | Inconsistent rendering; use inline stroke SVG |
| Purple/indigo gradients or glows | AI cliché. KIBO blue `#3446D4` is used flat, only on the phone and the logo |

## Type
| Use | Style |
|---|---|
| Phone | IBM Plex Sans KR, body ≥ 20px (≥ 16px for secondary lines); scale and weights in "Phone design" |
| Kiosk text | Every character ≥ 12 mm tall on a 27" panel: **≥ 40px for all text** on the 1080×1920 canvas, checked on the rendered glyph (Korean kiosk guidance, `docs/UI_QA.md`) |

## Components
- Touch targets ≥ 56×56px. Phone components are in "Phone design".
- Kiosk header: the store's mark (`BrandMark.tsx`: a cream speech bubble with a line drawing of what the store sells, burger or train, in the kiosk colour; never a capital letter on a coloured tile, which can recall a real chain, 14-final:2), the wordmark, and an 8px accent stripe under the bar.
- Kiosk cards (kiosk canvas): white, 32px radius, the soft two-layer shadow `CARD_SHADOW` (`src/components/kiosk/styles.ts`) instead of an outline; summary areas (cart bar, review and payment totals) are a white panel with a 48px rounded top and an upward shadow. An element showing the AI ring drops the card shadow (both are box-shadows).
- Product tile (kiosk canvas): photo 272px high, name 48px bold (a long English word or a 7-character Japanese part gets the size that fits, never under 40px), price 56px Space Grotesk in the kiosk colour (a Japanese "…ウォン" price the size that fits one line, about 46px) with kcal 40px secondary color beside it, badge top-left (BEST mustard, NEW teal), sold-out overlay with text. Every kiosk picture is fetched on K1 (`preloadKioskImages`), so none is blank when shown.
- K1 hero: the store's own offer from menu data (its NEW item, else BEST, with the tile price) on a white pill, shown only while the hero is at least 240px tall.
- Bottom sheet for set steps: step label ("Step 2 of 4 · set"), photo options in a 3-column grid, selected = 6px teal border + tint ring (kiosk canvas).
- Kiosk focus ring (kiosk canvas): 6px ink, offset 4px; the category rail's buttons, as wide as the rail that clips them, draw it inside, and the rail and the menu grid scroll keyboard focus clear of their bottom fade (#1 22).
- K3 cart chips (kiosk canvas): the line's name ×qty over its price (40px), like a real kiosk's cart; the options are on K6 and in KIBO's subtitle, so two lines fit the row whole (GitHub #1 item 1). When more lines than fit, the row scrolls sideways and fades out at its right edge.
- AI change: kiosk tile and cart chip get a teal 6–8px ring (kiosk canvas) for 2 s plus a status line "Added by AI: …". While the kiosk shows Korean, KIBO's English subtitle ("AI added: Cheeseburger set · Fries (M) · Zero cola") takes its own row under the toolbar for 6 s: KIBO blue `#3446D4` with white 40px text and a "KIBO" tag, so it never reads as the kiosk's own UI (14-final:0).
- KIBO working: from the moment the phone sends a message until the AI answers (at most 20 s), the toolbar row on K2, K3 and K6 shows a KIBO-blue pill with the "KIBO" tag and "처리하고 있어요 · Working…" (40px canvas), in place of the row's controls; the AI's own change line wins when both apply (14-final:1).
- Staff: the kiosk shows a banner when called (the phone's staff button is in "Phone design").
- Learned kiosk (`/learned/<kiosk>`, `/demo?k=doughhouse`, 14-final:3): the third-party page stays unchanged; KIBO's parts follow "Phone design" in CSS px. Beside the page a cream panel `clamp(320px, 28vw, 440px)`: what the page is (24px bold, English, then Korean in 16px muted), a white card with the QR (208px), "Or open **<host>** and enter" and the code (32px Space Grotesk), then the replay line (16px) and [Watch how KIBO learned it] (secondary) at the bottom; in `/demo` only the line, as a white strip under the page. Tap mark: a 56px KIBO-blue circle with a 4px white edge and the tap number (24px Space Grotesk, white), centred on the target, with one 400ms KIBO-blue ring spreading from it (`kibo-tap`; none with reduced motion). The line is KIBO blue nowhere: ink, semibold when done, a `staff-tint` box when the cart did not match.

## Phone design
Decided 10-13 (12-phone-app:0), after the user found the phone "clunky": calm, airy and app-like, with the clarity of a modern Korean finance or mobility app, but KIBO's own look (KIBO blue, cream, IBM Plex Sans KR, Space Grotesk for numbers). The elderly-first numbers do not move: body ≥ 20px, secondary ≥ 16px, targets ≥ 56px (primary ≥ 60px, mic and [Yes, pay now] 88px), text ≥ 4.5:1, focus always visible, color never alone. Components: `src/components/phone/`; class strings: `src/components/phone/ui.ts`; tokens: the phone `@theme` block in `src/app/globals.css`; preview with sample data: `/design/phone` (development only, 404 in production; `e2e/qa/design.qa.spec.ts`).

**Surfaces and elevation.** Cream page; white cards with `shadow-card` (two layers, 6% ink: `0 1px 2px` and `0 4px 16px`) and no border; the bottom dock and the P4 bar are white sheets with `shadow-dock` and a 26px top radius. A hairline (`line`, 1px) only separates rows inside a white card. **No 2px dark outlines anywhere**; the only dark edge is the 3px focus ring (`outline` ink, offset 2px; drawn inside, `FOCUS_INSIDE`, where the container clips the control's edge: the app bar's buttons, the `/demo` top bar and tabs, #1 22). On `/demo` the KIBO side follows the same rules (12-phone-app:1) inside the frame in "/demo frame": white column cards, white scenario cards, `kibo-tint` tags and sentence chips. While more is below, the guide's and "What the AI did"'s scroll areas end in a 32px fade to white (`ScrollFade`, a sticky overlay that takes no tap; not a CSS mask, which clips focus rings), so a cut line reads as "more below" (design QA 10-15). The guide and the phone's scroll area also start in the same 32px fade while content is above their top padding (`ScrollFade edge="top"`, over the content, under the phone's sheets), so a bubble, title or button cut at the top reads as "more above" (#1 32). The kiosk half keeps its own look, because it stands for an existing third-party kiosk.

**Radius.** `rounded-tile` 18px (buttons, bubbles, suggestions, fields), `rounded-sheet` 26px (cards, dock), pills `rounded-full`, cart thumbnails 12px.

**Buttons.** One filled primary per screen (I-142).
| Kind | Look |
|---|---|
| Primary | filled `kibo`, white 20px/700 text, full width, ≥ 60px (88px for [Yes, pay now]) |
| Secondary | `kibo-tint` with `kibo` 20px/600 text, no border, full width, ≥ 60px ([Pick more]) |
| Quiet | text button with an icon, `kibo` 20px/600, tint only while pressed, ≥ 56px ([Undo]) |
| Staff | `staff-tint` with `staff-ink`, bell icon over "Staff" / "직원", ≥ 56px; its accessible name is "Call staff" |
| Compact | icon over a 16px/600 label, ≥ 56×56px (app bar and dock side controls) |
Every button has pressed (color, and a 0.98 shrink only without reduced motion), disabled (60% opacity) and focus states.

**Icons.** `lucide-react` (ISC license), 24px, stroke 2, round caps and joins, always with a text label (mic 32px). Hand-drawn pictures (P5 card into the kiosk) use the same stroke style.

**Type.** At most 4 sizes per screen: 16 / 20 / 24 / 32px; the order number (120px) does not count. Numbers (totals, prices, order number) in Space Grotesk 700 with tabular figures where they line up. Weights: titles 700, labels and line names 600, body 400. Body line height 1.5.

**App bar** (every phone screen). One row, 64px, white with `shadow-card`: the KIBO mark (32px), the kiosk brand (16px/700) over a dot and a word ("Connected" / "Reconnecting…", 16px muted; the dot is never the only signal), then the language button (globe over the other language's full name, "한국어" / "English": a short code like "EN" would not be part of its accessible name, WCAG 2.5.3), then the staff button. No visible borders. On a phone ≤ 380px wide with larger text the bar gives the mark's room to the brand, which is one step smaller there (14px × 1.15, still ≥ 16px), so it breaks only between words ("MALO BURGER" on one line, "SKYLINE / AIRPORT RAIL" on two; I-341, #1 21). On `/assistant` and the `/demo` phone (14-final:4) the language button shows the language in use in its own words ("日本語", at most 128px, truncated) under a globe and an "Aa" icon, is named "Language and text size: 日本語", and opens the "Language and text size" sheet (15-judge-fixes:2): a cream layer over the phone with a 64px white bar (title 24px/700, quiet [Close]), first "Text size" with a [Larger text] switch row in a white card (and, on a phone ≤ 380px wide with larger text, an AI voice switch card, which the dock gives up there; the app bar has no room for a fourth button), then two groups under 16px/600 muted headings, each language a 60px white card with `shadow-card` (native name 20px/600 with `lang`, English name 16px muted under it; the one in use `kibo-tint` with a check mark), and a 16px muted note that the seven AI-only languages are checked by typing only. Focus starts on the language in use; the rest is inert; Escape closes. With an AI-only language the few translated strings carry their own `lang`, and cart lines show the Korean name (20px/600) over the English name (16px muted).

**Conversation.** User bubble right in `kibo-bubble` with ink text; AI bubble left, white with `shadow-card`; both 20px with one squared corner toward the speaker; notices full width in `staff-tint`. Only the last two messages are large; older ones are 16px behind "Earlier messages". After each send and reply the newest message and the choices under it scroll fully into view above the dock (their top first when they do not fit); on P4 the AI's reply sits above the card, not under the bottom sheet (15-judge-fixes:2). State pills ("Listening…", "Working…", "Reconnecting…") are small 16px/600 pills centred above the dock, white with `shadow-card` (warnings in `staff-tint`). The phone's scroll area (every phone screen, on `/assistant` and `/demo`) ends in a 32px fade to cream while more is below (`ScrollFade`, as in the scenario guide), so a line cut at the dock reads as "more below" (#1 2-phone).

**Cart card.** Header "Your order · 2 items" (title 20px/700, count 400 muted; the count is the sum of quantities) with a quiet [Undo]. Rows: 48px thumbnail (none on a learned kiosk, I-103), name 20px/600 on one or two lines, options 16px muted, price right-aligned 20px/600 with a 16px quantity pill under it. Total row under a hairline: "Total" 20px/600 and the amount 32px Space Grotesk. On the phone and on `/demo` (#1 18: right after the first order, `/demo`'s full dock left the total out of view at every laptop size), while the card is out of view (a small phone, larger text, a long question above it, a laptop's short column) and no choice buttons show (it would cover them, 15-judge-fixes:2), a one-line summary sits above the dock, 8px clear of the content: a white pill with `shadow-card`, "Your order 1 item · ₩7,200" / "내 주문 1개 · 7,200원" in 20px/600 `kibo-deep` and a chevron, ≥ 56px, on one line: with larger text, or when the pill is narrower than 19rem, only "1 item · ₩7,200" shows and screen readers still hear "Your order" (#1 9); it wraps rather than cuts text when a user's text spacing needs more room (WCAG 1.4.12); a tap scrolls to the card's total (14-final:1).

**Choices.** White cards with a 48px photo and the name (20px/600), then " · 5,200원" in muted from menu data (an item's tile price, or "+500원" for an option that adds to it; none for a set choice, a fare or a free option). An offer the user may turn down (the dessert upsell) ends with [No thanks] / [괜찮아요] in `kibo-tint`; it sends the answer to the AI and the buttons go at once.

**Suggestions.** "Try saying" (16px/600 muted) over soft `kibo-tint` cards with a speech-bubble icon, 20px `kibo-deep` text (the phone body size, not 18px), no border; a tap sends the sentence. On `/demo` they sit above the cart card while the cart is empty, so a laptop shows them above the dock (#1 11).

**Bottom dock** (P3). A white sheet, ≤ 150px tall at 390×844: an 88px round mic in the middle with what a tap does under it ("Tap to talk"; "Tap to cancel" while connecting or reconnecting; "Tap to end voice" while listening, thinking or speaking), its state named first for screen readers; on the left a 56px [Type] (keyboard icon) that opens the text field and [Send] above the row (200ms rise) and becomes [Hide] (keyboard-off icon, named "Hide keyboard": "Close" read as ending the order, 15-judge-fixes:2); on the right a 56px AI voice switch (not on a phone ≤ 380px wide with larger text: it moves to the app bar's sheet) (speaker icon over "Voice on" / "Voice off", named "AI voice"). The mic is filled blue at rest, white with a 3px blue ring while voice runs (plus the listening pulse), and tinted while the text field is open, so [Send] is the one filled button. While the text field has focus on the phone (the on-screen keyboard is up), the mic shrinks to a 56px circle without its label (the words stay for screen readers) and the side buttons lose their top margin; `/demo` keeps the full dock (14-final:1) and, when the cart card is out of view, the cart summary above it (#1 18). What is happening shows as a pill above the dock ("Listening…", "Working…" from the moment a message is sent, the `/demo` voice timer); why voice stopped, or the noise hint, is one warning line at the top of the dock. The text field stays open for the rest of the order once opened (by [Type], [Type instead] on P1, or voice giving way to text) until [Hide]; `/demo` opens it at the start, because a judge on a laptop usually types. After the `/demo` message limit, the limit line and [Try again] take its place.

**Confirm (P4).** The scrolling part: a white card with "Please check your order" (24px/700), the dine option (20px muted) and the lines, then [Pick more] (secondary). The fixed bottom sheet: the total (32px), [Yes, pay now] (88px, filled) and a row of [Type] and a tinted mic pill (88px, "Tap to talk" over "You can also say yes."), so the total and the button are always fully visible (I-134). On a learned kiosk there is no bottom sheet: the total is in the card, the kiosk's own checkout hint sits between the card and [Pick more], and the dock stays.

No button: on `/demo` the card stand-in is right under the kiosk and as wide as it, "Kiosk: insert card (demo)" in the kiosk's accent colour with ink 16px/700 text and a card icon, 56px tall on a 4px strip of the kiosk's dark frame (`#141210`), ink focus ring drawn inside (15-judge-fixes:2, #1 E).

**Done (P6).** One white card: "Order number" (20px/600 muted), the number 120px Space Grotesk in KIBO blue with proportional figures and 0.04em tracking (the tabular "1" has a foot, and a 7 and a 1 touched), "We'll call your number at the counter."; [Done] (primary) under the card (on `/demo` the end card instead).

**Home (P0, `/app`).** No app bar (there is no kiosk yet): the KIBO logo (40px) with a 56px [Larger text] switch beside it (Aa icon over the words; a check mark and `kibo-tint` when on; 15-judge-fixes:2), one 24px line, [Continue my order] (secondary) only while this phone holds an open order, then the pairing card (12-phone-app:3, the primary way in): "Enter the 6-digit code shown on the kiosk" (20px/600), a 72px numeric field with 32px Space Grotesk digits spaced 0.3em and a 1px `field` edge (its hint "6 digits" / "숫자 6자리" in 20px regular, unspaced), the error under it in `staff-ink` 20px, and [Connect] (primary, 88px, link icon); then "Or connect with the QR" (16px muted) over [Scan a kiosk] (secondary, scan icon); then two white cards: "How it works" (three numbered steps on `kibo-tint` circles) and "Settings": the language as two halves on a tint track (the chosen one white with a check mark), and full-width switch rows for AI voice and larger text (name left; "On"/"Off" in words and a 56×32 track right, `kibo` on, `field` off). Larger text sets the root font size to 115%, so every rem size grows and the browser's own text size still applies.

**Scanner (`/app/scan`).** [Back] (quiet) and the 24px title; first the camera hint like the microphone hint (a white card picturing the browser's prompt with [Allow] ringed, "Tap **Allow** on the next screen.", [Turn on the camera] primary 88px); then the camera, square, 26px radius, with a white 4px framing square and the outside dimmed, "Point at the QR on the kiosk" (20px) under it, a `staff-tint` line for a QR that is not a KIBO kiosk, and [Light on] (secondary small) only when the camera has a torch. **Paused layer** (12-phone-app:7, `src/components/PausedLayer.tsx`). After 20 min with nothing happening the phone or `/demo` stops reading the order: one full-screen button on 95% cream, a 96px `kibo-tint` circle with the pause icon in KIBO blue, then "Paused · Tap to continue" (24px/700 ink) in the UI language. Kiosk screens never show it (I-172). It fades in over 200ms (`kibo-fade-in`, no movement, so reduced motion needs nothing else), takes focus, and makes the page under it inert; the whole tap resumes.

**Motion.** Sheets open in 200ms ease-out (`kibo-sheet-in`; a plain fade with reduced motion); presses change color in 150ms; the listening pulse is unchanged. Nothing else moves.

## Motion

## Accessibility
- Every control is a real `<button>`/`<a>`/`<input>` with a label; icon-only buttons get `aria-label`.
- Cart changes announced with `aria-live` when AI voice is off; suppressed while AI is speaking.
- While a sheet or dialog is open, the rest of the screen is `inert`, so focus never lands under the scrim (kiosk: `ModalLayer`).
- Respect system font size; no text in images.
